01About

Most data problems are really question problems.

I'm a data analyst focused on SEO and growth analytics. My job is to figure out which number actually answers the question a team is trying to ask — then report it clearly enough that people know what to do next.

I'm a recent Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science) graduate, with honors in AI & ML, and I currently work as an SEO Analyst at Feedspot. There I build and maintain the dashboards and KPI reports the product and growth teams rely on — tracking traffic, signups, churn, conversion, and user behaviour across markets.

I like work that's reproducible and easy to trust: clean SQL, documented metrics, and dashboards that hold up. Charts are the easy part — a clear recommendation is the deliverable.
